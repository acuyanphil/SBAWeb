Junior Business Executives

Business Administration Organization

SC – JBEX serves as a medium of expression for business students & ideas and aspirations, protects and promotes the welfare and interests of business students, asserts dynamic and responsible student leadership, fosters moral, social, intellectual, and spiritual growth, and strengthens unifying ties among members.

Core Purpose & Objectives:

  • Camaraderie & Unity: Promote unity and camaraderie among business students both within the campus and with other institutions.
  • Educational Advancement: Adopt and develop measures necessary for the academic advancement of members in the business field.
  • Discipline & Awareness: Develop public awareness and discipline among members.
  • Practical Business Exposure: Serve as a bridge for continuing student exposure to actual business practices and acquaint members with professional business activities.

Junior Philippine Institute Accountants

Core Purpose & Objectives:

  • Academic Excellence: Maintain high standards of scholarship in accounting studies.
